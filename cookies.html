Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how JamboFlow LLC uses cookies and similar technologies on its website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve functionality, and provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site, improve performance, and support basic analytics. We do not use cookies to store personal information or track users for advertising purposes.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may limit certain functionality of our website.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features of our site may use third-party tools (such as embedded forms or analytics) that may also use cookies. These third parties are responsible for their own cookie practices.

5. Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@jamboflow.com.
