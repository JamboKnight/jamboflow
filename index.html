The entire lead intake and follow-up sequence was rebuilt end-to-end — from first contact to booked call. The system responds in minutes. It has not missed a lead since launch.

3 hours → under 8 minutes.

Every new client engagement required three-plus hours of manual setup — contracts, briefs, calendar coordination, account access.

Onboarding was rebuilt as a single automated flow triggered the moment a proposal is accepted. The team now spends those hours on the work itself.

We built the integration from the ground up. It has run continuously, without human input, since the day it launched. The client has not touched it.

In Practice

Every business has a
version of this problem.

The specific details change. The pattern — manual work where a system should be — doesn't.

Trades &
Contractors

A roofing contractor was losing estimate jobs to faster-responding competitors. Their first reply was going out the next morning — sometimes two days later.

→Automated enquiry response and site-visit booking. Every new lead gets a reply within minutes and a calendar link — without anyone touching the keyboard.

Professional
Practice

A physiotherapy clinic was spending 40 minutes per new patient on intake — manually emailing forms, chasing returns, then entering the data by hand.

→An intake flow triggered at the point of booking. Forms go out, responses come back, and the patient record is built before anyone walks through the door.

E-Commerce

A DTC brand founder was manually compiling weekly performance numbers from Shopify, their email platform, and two ad accounts every Monday morning.

→A single automated report — every metric formatted and delivered to their inbox before 8am, every Monday, without touching a spreadsheet.

Agency

A creative agency was onboarding every new client through a string of separately-sent emails, documents, Slack invitations, and calendar holds — all done by hand, one by one.

→One trigger: contract signed. Every welcome email, doc link, channel invite, and kickoff calendar hold runs automatically from that point forward.

Service
Business

A personal training studio was following up with trial clients three days after their session — by which point most had already decided whether to continue.

→A post-session sequence timed to follow up when interest is highest, not when someone remembers to send it. Manual effort: zero.
